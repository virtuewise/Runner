# RUNNER Product Vision

> RUNNER isn't about what you're carrying. It's about getting it there.

> Every mission has a destination. Every destination needs a Runner.

## Vision

Build a peer-to-peer movement network coordinating requesters, independently qualified Runners, multi-hop relay legs and verifiable custody from origin to destination.

## Participants

Requester defines the mission; Runner performs a leg; dispatcher coordinates capacity and exceptions; trust operator handles safety and disputes; partners may provide hubs or transport capacity. Personas remain hypotheses pending field research.

## Differentiation hypotheses

Mission-centric orchestration, multi-hop relay, accountable custody, capability/geography eligibility, risk-aware dispatch and transparent mission state. These are hypotheses, not proven competitive advantages.

## Principles

Destination outcome over activity metrics. Safety and legal compliance over speed. Trust requires evidence. Every state transition is authorized and audited. A planned route is not a promise until every leg is feasible. Degrade safely during outages.

## Launch-market hypothesis

Johannesburg/Gauteng is a candidate geography, not a confirmed commercial commitment. Validate demand, Runner density, insurance, law, item categories and unit economics.

## North star

Verified successful mission completions per active requester cohort, guarded by safety, claims, on-time completion and contribution margin.

## Initial non-goals

Unrestricted item categories, global launch before local operations are proven, automated legal/customs decisions, unsupported delivery guarantees and speculative infrastructure.
