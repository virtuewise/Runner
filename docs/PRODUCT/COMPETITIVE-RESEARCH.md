# Competitive Research

Status: Research framework; no market claims verified. Owner: Product Strategy.

## Categories to compare

On-demand couriers, peer-to-peer delivery marketplaces, parcel networks, freight forwarders and dispatch software.

## Scorecard

Score 1–5 with source and confidence: mission coverage, multi-hop orchestration, custody verification, Runner qualification/insurance, ETA reliability, exception recovery, geography, price transparency, UX and unit economics.

## Positioning hypothesis

RUNNER differentiates through accountable mission orchestration across independently qualified legs, not simply sender-to-courier matching.

## Research protocol

Interview at least 15 requesters and 15 potential Runners in the candidate market; collect five recurring business workflows; date and cite every competitor claim; refresh quarterly. Do not publish first/only/fastest/cheapest claims without evidence.
