# RUNNER product foundation

## The promise

**RUNNER isn't about what you're carrying. It's about getting it there.**

*Every mission has a destination. Every destination needs a Runner.*

RUNNER is a movement and a trusted global network—not a courier app with a new coat of paint. Being a Runner is an identity and an economic opportunity built on reliability, accountability and service.

## Product principles

- **Mission-first:** the unit of work is a mission with explicit terms and accountability.
- **Open opportunity, earned trust:** participation is accessible, but mission eligibility is earned and verified.
- **Dual-sided identity:** an account can request a mission and separately qualify to fulfil missions.
- **Choice with guardrails:** direct matching, controlled open missions and offers can coexist under policy.
- **Universal movement:** support different transport modes and mission sizes when legal, safe and operationally viable.
- **Trust is product:** verification, custody, proof, insurance options, support and dispute resolution are core workflows.
- **Local execution, global standard:** service areas, legal restrictions, pricing and operating rules are jurisdiction-aware.

## Initial personas

1. Requester: an individual who needs a permitted item moved.
2. Runner: a qualified person who fulfils eligible missions.
3. Business operator: an organization dispatching and reconciling recurring missions.
4. Trust operator: a human reviewer handling verification, exceptions, safety cases and disputes.

## Launch wedge

Start in one dense operating area with a narrow set of low-risk categories: documents, ordinary parcels and retail collections with clear size/weight limits. Validate supply density, successful handoffs, repeat usage, unit economics and incident rates before expanding categories or geographies.

Do not launch with "anything" literally. Exclude prohibited goods and require policy-specific handling for regulated, hazardous, high-value, fragile, age-restricted or temperature-sensitive items.

## First release milestones

- **M0 — Foundation:** product language, design system, universal client, domain contracts, CI and security baseline.
- **M1 — Trusted request:** account onboarding, mission creation, quote transparency and prohibited-item screening.
- **M2 — Qualified supply:** Runner onboarding, identity and capability checks, service areas and availability.
- **M3 — Accountable fulfilment:** assignment, custody handoffs, proof of delivery, support and disputes.
- **M4 — Reliable operations:** payments, ledger reconciliation, realtime updates, analytics and business console.
- **M5 — Network effects:** referrals, Runner progression, reputation, repeat business and new regions.

## Metrics that matter

- Successful mission completion rate.
- On-time completion rate, segmented by mission class.
- Pickup-to-delivery custody exception rate.
- Safety incidents and substantiated disputes per 1,000 missions.
- Request-to-assignment time and Runner utilization.
- Repeat requester rate and Runner retention.
- Contribution margin per completed mission.
- Verification completion and fraud loss rates.

Do not optimize growth at the expense of safety, worker fairness or reliable service.
